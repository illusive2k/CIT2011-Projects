In the chapter "Seven Cures for a Lean Purse" from the book "The Richest Man in Babylon," the author George S. Clason presents seven principles for achieving financial success and building wealth by knowing what to do with the money you earn.Arkhad gives some principles to follow in this chapter,
1. Start thy purse fattening which means to save at least 10% of your earnings.
2. Control thy expenditures which means to live below your means and avoid unnecessary expenses.
3. Make thy gold multiply which means to invest your savings wisely to generate more income.
4. Guard thy treasures from loss which means to protect your investments and avoid risky ventures
5. Insure a future income, this means you should be preparing financially for the future.
6.Increase thy ability to earn, Improve your knowledge and skills so your earning potential increases over time.


To be honest I could use these principles in my day to day life as a full time university student. Everytime I recieve money from my mother, everything goes straight to spending with no savings or thought of thinking for the future. The principle that was the most relevant to me was the one that says "Increase thy ability to earn" as I am a student and I have to be able to increase my earning potential in the future. I can do this by improving my knowledge and skills in my field of study so that I can be more valuable to potential employers. This will help me to earn more money in the future and be able to save and invest for my future financial security as a uni students.

"The Five Laws of Gold" focuses more on how to protect and grow money once you have it. It says to put away at least a tenth of your earnings for future and family. As a student I dont think about the future financially alot, I mostly worry about the present day and how I will survive during the day.

The book further argues that money should be placed into productive opportunities where it can generate additional income. After reading this portion, it made me realize that saving was only the beginning of whats to come and just not the finished product>

The chapter also says money can be lost when people invest in business or purposes they are unfamiliar with. Things like trading stocks and foriegn exchange can look good on the media because of the persons insane success but in reality its alot of risk and hardwork involved that most people dont see and without proper knowlegde you can lose alot of money.

My biggest takeaway from these two chapters is that having money alone is not enough to be financially stable, mone with purpose and knowledge behind it is the key to manage it succesfully so that you can retain it and even grow your profits off it to become successful in the future. I have learned that I need to be more mindful of my spending habits and start saving for the future. I also need to educate myself on financial literacy and investment opportunities so that I can make informed decisions about my money. Overall, these chapters have taught me the importance of being financially responsible and planning for the future.
